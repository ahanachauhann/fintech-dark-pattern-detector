The Key Fact Statement must disclose the total interest charge during the entire tenor of the loan, in Rupees, as a single clearly stated figure.

Other up-front charges, if any, must be disclosed with a break-up of each component, including processing fees and insurance charges, given separately rather than bundled into one figure.

The net disbursed amount to the borrower equals the loan amount minus all up-front charges, and this net figure must be shown to the borrower distinctly from the gross loan amount.

The total amount to be paid by the borrower over the life of the loan, being the sum of the loan amount, total interest, and all up-front charges, must be disclosed as a single annualized percentage rate figure for ease of comparison across lenders.

The rate of annualized penal charges in case of delayed payments, if any, must be disclosed upfront in the Key Fact Statement, along with the rate of any other annualized penal charges that may apply.

Contact details of the nodal grievance redressal officer designated specifically to deal with digital lending related complaints must be disclosed to the borrower.

Penal charges will be based on the outstanding amount of the loan and shall be reasonable and non-discriminatory, and standardized across similar product categories, rather than compounded or calculated as penal interest added to the regular interest rate.

No charge or fee other than those disclosed in the Key Fact Statement at the time of sanction shall be charged by the lender to the borrower during the loan tenor without the borrower's explicit consent.

Processing fees, charged once before disbursal, foreclosure charges, and part-prepayment charges must each be disclosed as a distinct line item rather than folded into a single combined "other charges" figure.

Disbursement of the loan amount would be made post deduction of the processing fee, insurance charges, and any other applicable charges, meaning the amount credited to the borrower's account may be meaningfully lower than the sanctioned loan amount.
