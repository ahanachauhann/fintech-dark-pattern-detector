All fees and charges applicable to the loan must be clearly disclosed to the borrower upfront in the Key Fact Statement and loan agreement, and no fee outside this disclosure may be levied at any later stage of the loan tenor.

Penal charges shall be reasonable, non-discriminatory, and standardized across similar product categories, and shall not be compounded or added to the regular interest rate charged on the loan.

Penal charges will be clearly disclosed in the loan agreement, Key Fact Statement, and via SMS, email, or app notifications at the time they are levied, rather than left for the borrower to discover only upon reviewing a statement.

No penal charges will be levied in case of disputes, technical failures, or delays arising from force majeure events outside the borrower's control.

The Annual Percentage Rate includes the interest rate, processing fee, documentation charges, and insurance if bundled, and any other fee known at the time of sanction, and shall be calculated using a standardized formula rather than left to the lender's discretion.

A digitally signed Key Fact Statement shall be mandatorily provided to the borrower before disbursal, and shall clearly mention all fees, charges, interest rate, Annual Percentage Rate, penal charges, recovery mechanisms, and the cooling-off period, among other key terms.

Processing fees are charged once before disbursal and are non-refundable, including in cases where the loan is cancelled shortly after disbursal.

Daily penal charges are optional for demand loans and, where applied, compound on a daily basis for every day the repayment remains outstanding past the due date.

Charges for switching between loan products, statement re-issuance, mandate cancellation, and cheque re-issuance are each levied separately and are not included within the headline interest rate quoted to the borrower at the time of sanction.
