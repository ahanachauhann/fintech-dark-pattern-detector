As per the terms and conditions outlined in the Transaction Documents, the Borrower is obligated to repay the Loan, including interest, fees, and other charges, by the due dates specified in the repayment schedule.

The Lenders will have the right to share the Borrower's default details with Credit Information Companies (CICs), and a default history against the Borrower's name could jeopardize the Borrower's future creditworthiness.

The Lenders may, as per standard terms and conditions, recall the entire loan amount advanced in view of any default committed by the Borrower in repaying the EMIs as per the repayment schedule.

Failure to pay the overdue amount within the specified notice period may result in the lenders sharing default details with credit agencies and initiating legal proceedings for recovery, without further individual notice to the Borrower.

The Borrower entered into a Loan agreement and other related Transaction Documents with the Lenders at the time of loan disbursal, and continued use of the loan facility constitutes acceptance of all terms contained therein, including those incorporated by reference.
