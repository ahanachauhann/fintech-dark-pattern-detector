We must be informed of any event or occurrence that may give rise to a claim under this Policy within 14 days of the diagnosis of the first occurrence.

An Insured Person must submit the requisite claim documents and information establishing the circumstances of the claim, its quantum, and Our liability for the claim within 60 days of the diagnosis, failing which the claim may not be payable unless the Insured Person has complied with all obligations under this Policy.

We will not cover any person above the age of 66 for an annual policy and 67 for a 2-year policy.

If the policy is renewed within 15 days from the due date of renewal, the policy cover shall continue without a break; however, any claim incurred during the break-in period will not be payable under this policy.

This Policy constitutes the complete contract of insurance. This Policy cannot be changed or varied by anyone, including an insurance agent or broker, except Us, and any change We make will be communicated in writing.

This Policy will automatically terminate at the end of the Policy Period and We are under no obligation to notify the Insured Person of such termination.

Any act or omission made in a dishonest or fraudulent manner under or in relation to this Policy or the renewal of the Policy poses a moral hazard and may result in the Policy being voided.

No insurance agents, brokers, or other person or entity is authorised to receive premium on Our behalf or to make any representation or promise on Our behalf that is not contained in this Policy wording.

Any recovery We make pursuant to this clause shall first be applied to the amounts paid or payable by Us under this Policy and Our costs and expenses of effecting such recovery, before any balance is paid to the Insured Person.

The free health check-up benefit under a family floater policy cannot be availed by any member if any single member covered under the policy has raised a claim during the preceding four policy years.
